Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

This policy explains how the Montclare Children's School Parents Association ("the PA," "we," "us") handles information collected through the Montclare Holiday Fair website at holidayfair.montclarepa.org.

What we collect

Face-painting waitlist: your name, your child's name, your mobile number, and a record of when you agreed to receive text messages.

50/50 raffle: the name you enter, an optional phone number or email (used only to reach you if you win after you've left), the number of tickets, the amount, how you paid (card or cash), and your ticket numbers. Card payments are processed in person by Square. We receive a Square transaction ID, never your card number.

On your device: the site stores a random code in your browser so it can show your own waitlist ticket and raffle orders. We don't use tracking cookies, analytics, or ads.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the waitlist and the raffle at the fair: to call your child's turn, to record ticket sales, to draw and contact the winner, and to reply to you.

Text messages

Face-painting "turn ready" texts are sent on our behalf by J&Z Foundation Inc. through a text-messaging service provider, which handles your number only to deliver those messages. The J&Z Foundation Terms and Privacy Policy also apply to these texts. Reply STOP to any message to opt out.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We do not share phone numbers or text-messaging opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention

Waitlist entries, including phone numbers, are deleted after the fair. Raffle entries are deleted after the prize is paid out and the totals are recorded.

Questions

Email treasurer@montclarepa.org.
