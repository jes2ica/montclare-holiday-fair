Montclare Children's School Holiday Fair · Organized by the holiday fair committee

Admission · Second Chance Toys

Montclare Gives Back

This year, Montclare Gives Back is teaming up with Second Chance Toys for our Holiday Fair, Yeti, Set, Snow! Admission to the fair is a donation of gently used plastic toys, which will be cleaned up and passed along to children in need, giving each toy a second chance to bring someone joy this holiday season.

Plastic toys often look almost new even after years of play, yet many end up in landfills, where they take up space and never break down. At the same time, many children across the country have few or no toys of their own. Second Chance Toys bridges that gap by rescuing gently used plastic toys and donating them to community organizations that serve children in need.

Can't make it to the fair? Toy collection will continue through December 9, so there's still time to give a toy a second chance!

Montclare Gives Back is a parent-led committee dedicated to helping our families make a difference in the wider community. Throughout the school year, we host drives that collect coats, books, food, toys, and other essentials for those in need. We're proud to partner with organizations such as NY Cares, Project Cicero, Homeward, Second Chance Toys and local shelters to make sure every donation reaches the people who need it most.

None of this would be possible without the kindness and generosity of Montclare families. Each drive is a chance for our children to see firsthand how small acts of giving add up, and to learn that caring for our neighbors is part of being a community.
