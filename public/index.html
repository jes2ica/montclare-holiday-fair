Admission · Second Chance Toys

Montclare Gives Back

This year, Montclare Gives Back is teaming up with Second Chance Toys for our Holiday Fair, Yeti, Set, Snow! Admission to the fair is a donation of gently used plastic toys, which will be cleaned up and passed along to children in need, giving each toy a second chance to bring someone joy this holiday season.

Plastic toys often look almost new even after years of play, yet many end up in landfills, where they take up space and never break down. At the same time, many children across the country have few or no toys of their own. Second Chance Toys bridges that gap by rescuing gently used plastic toys and donating them to community organizations that serve children in need.

Can't make it to the fair? Toy collection will continue through December 9, so there's still time to give a toy a second chance!

Montclare Gives Back is a parent-led committee dedicated to helping our families make a difference in the wider community. Throughout the school year, we host drives that collect coats, books, food, toys, and other essentials for those in need. We're proud to partner with organizations such as NY Cares, Project Cicero, Homeward, Second Chance Toys and local shelters to make sure every donation reaches the people who need it most.

None of this would be possible without the kindness and generosity of Montclare families. Each drive is a chance for our children to see firsthand how small acts of giving add up, and to learn that caring for our neighbors is part of being a community.

Gym 2 · Montclare Scholarship Fund

Scholarship Drive

Tis the season of giving, and there's no better gift than a great start. Every year, the MPA raises money for the Montclare Scholarship Fund, which supports families who would not be able to send their children to our amazing school without help.

You've seen the Montclare magic firsthand. Studies show that high-quality early childhood education can change the entire trajectory of a child's life, with better outcomes that extend throughout adulthood.

Our wish this year: open the doors to more children. ✨

On average, only 7% of Montclare families currently receive assistance. Those of you with older children in independent schools will know that those schools often have 20%–40% of students receiving financial aid. And every year, more families apply for aid than we can fund. That's why every contribution, even $20, makes such a real difference.

Why your gift goes further

  • 100% goes to financial aid. Every dollar goes directly to Montclare families, and funds do not support any other projects or initiatives.
  • 100% tax-deductible. The MPA is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization.
  • Employer match eligible. Your donation may qualify for an employer or corporate match.
  • Doubled by Cadence Education. Most importantly, every contribution is matched 100% by Cadence, doubling your impact.

A little holiday math: a $100 donation with a 100% corporate match becomes $200, and with Cadence's match it ultimately becomes $400! 🎄

Our Scholarship Drive kicked off earlier this month on Giving Tuesday, December 1. Help us make the season bright for a future Montclare family!
